## Notes for you (do not publish)

1. **Order matters.** The page tells one story: *who I am, what I can do for you, proof I can do it, how we will work, how to reach me.* Keep that order.
2. **Check the permissions.** Before showing client names and screenshots, make sure each client agrees. For nPanel, check that Nisc Cloud is happy for you to show it.
3. **Check every number.** All figures here are the ones you gave me. Keep them the same as your CV and your application note, because people compare them.
4. **Fill the brackets.** A portfolio with empty brackets looks unfinished. If you do not have a link or a quote yet, remove that line rather than leave a placeholder.
5. **Make it fast and phone-friendly.** Most visitors in Kenya will open it on a phone.
6. **Add Play Store link, GitHub and real screenshots first.** These are proof people can check in seconds, and they matter more than any sentence you write about yourself.
